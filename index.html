    Before you use this guide

    Bedside Drug Guide is a quick reference for nurses and nursing students. It doesn’t replace current prescribing information, your pharmacist, clinical judgment, or your institution’s policies and protocols.

    Doses are typical adult doses unless an entry says otherwise. Verify the dose, route and rate before giving any medication.
